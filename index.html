Data & Game Intelligence

Structured Gameplay Data, Beyond Traditional QA

Alongside conventional testing, we support structured capture and logging workflows that preserve gameplay context for analysis, evaluation, and research-oriented use cases.

01

Multimodal Game Recording

Capture synchronized gameplay video, audio, and relevant in-game context across testing sessions to create richer evidence than screenshots alone.

02

Telemetry Logging

Record structured gameplay events and state changes alongside testing activity to provide additional context for debugging, measurement, and analysis.

03

Action-Conditioned Trajectory Collection

Collect gameplay trajectories linked to defined player actions and relevant conditions, creating contextual data for evaluation, analytics, and advanced testing workflows.

Designed for structured, contextual capture.

These capabilities can complement QA by connecting what happened on screen with the actions, events, and game states surrounding it. We do not claim AI-training or research outcomes unless a specific engagement defines and validates them.

What a ZeusaberZ Bug Report Looks Like

Every issue we find is documented with the detail your engineers need to reproduce it, understand it, and fix it — the first time. This is a sample report for demonstration purposes only.

  2. Sprint toward Warehouse Gate
  3. Jump while turning left
  4. Observe character collision

Who We Are

About ZeusaberZ Testing

Testing Every Detail. Elevating Every Game.

ZeusaberZ Testing is an independent game testing and quality assurance organization focused on helping game teams identify issues before they reach players.

We believe great games are built through iteration, attention to detail, and rigorous testing. Our approach combines structured QA methodology with real-world player perspective to uncover problems that can affect gameplay, performance, reliability, and overall experience.

We don't just look for bugs. We look for the details that can make or break a player's experience.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

A concise overview of how ZeusaberZ Testing handles information submitted through this website.

Information We Collect

When you contact ZeusaberZ Testing through the inquiry form, we may receive the information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, company or studio, game or project name, platform, testing requirements, and message.

How We Use It

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, understand requested testing services, communicate about potential engagements, and provide relevant follow-up.

Form Processing

Website inquiries are submitted through our third-party form delivery provider, FormSubmit, which processes the form data so it can be delivered to our contact email address. Their handling of submitted information is governed by their own policies.

What You Should Not Submit

Please do not use the public inquiry form to send confidential source code, unreleased builds, credentials, proprietary datasets, or other sensitive materials. We can establish an appropriate secure transfer method when a project requires one.

Data Requests

If you have questions about information submitted through this website or want to request clarification about its handling, contact us at contact@zeusaberz.com.
